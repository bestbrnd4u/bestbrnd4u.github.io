💯 Authenticity

Why the items in our catalog are authentic

Every item is purchased on the brand's official US website and shipped to Ukraine. There's no reselling between the brand's store and the buyer — and it's exactly where the chain is long and unknown that fakes appear.

How it's set up FAQ
🛫

The item's journey: two links instead of five

We know where each item comes from right from the start, because the start is the brand's official store.

1

Purchase on the brand's official website

Each item is bought where the brand sells it directly — in its own online store in the US. It's a regular purchase from the brand, not from a reseller or a classifieds site.

2

Delivery to Ukraine

Then the item travels to Ukraine and reaches the buyer. It isn't resold along the way, repackaged, or “re-completed” — it arrives exactly as it left the brand's store.

🔍

How this differs from regular resale

❓

When the origin is unknown

On classifieds sites and in secondhand resale, an item passes through an unknown number of owners. The buyer is left to verify authenticity from photos — by the stitching, tags, logo font. That's work they never signed up for, and it's easy to get wrong.

📍

When the origin is known

Here, the question "is it authentic?" comes down to "where was it bought?" It was bought from the brand — so it's authentic. Nobody has to inspect the stitching under a magnifying glass.

🚫

What we don't sell

It's worth saying plainly, because this is exactly what usually ends up in the fine print.

✕

Replicas — not even “high-quality” or “1:1” ones.

✕

Items "just like the original" — no “luxury replicas” or “AAA-grade” copies.

✕

Items of unknown origin — items bought by someone else for resale.

The catalog has no separate category for copies, and there's never "the same model, only cheaper." If the model you want isn't in stock, you can order it through personal shopper, and it will be purchased just like the rest.
