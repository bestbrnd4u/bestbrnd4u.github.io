🚚 Payment & delivery

Convenient payment and fast delivery across Ukraine

Pay by card online or on delivery — it's your choice. We ship orders within 1–2 days, and Nova Poshta delivers to any city in Ukraine in 1–3 days.

Payment methods Delivery methods
💳

Payment methods

Choose how to pay at checkout — by card online or when you receive the parcel.

💳

Card payment online

Visa or Mastercard — secure online payment with no fees. A manager will contact you to confirm the order and payment.

📦

Payment on delivery

Pay in cash or by card at the Nova Poshta branch or to the courier — you only pay when you pick up your order.

🚚

Delivery methods

We deliver across Ukraine via Nova Poshta — choose the pickup option that suits you.

Delivery method Carrier rate Time frame, days
📦 Nova Poshta, branch at the carrier's rates 1–3
🏤 Nova Poshta, parcel locker at the carrier's rates 1–3
🚪 Nova Poshta, courier to your door at the carrier's rates 1–3
📮 Ukrposhta at the carrier's rates 2–5

Orders from 30 000 UAH are delivered free of charge: the shop pays for shipping by any method. Other orders ship at the carrier's rates.

You pay the carrier for delivery upon receipt — at their rate, which depends on the weight and size of the shipment. The order total on the site doesn't include delivery: it covers only the items.

🥇

How to place an order

Four steps from the item you chose to the parcel in your hands.

1

Choose an item

Add the item to your cart, choosing color and size, and proceed to checkout.

2

Enter delivery details

You fill in your contact details and choose a city and Nova Poshta branch, or courier delivery to your door.

3

Choose a payment method

Pay by card online or on delivery — whichever suits you.

4

Receive your order

We ship orders within 1–2 days. You'll get a tracking number and can pick up your parcel at a convenient time.
