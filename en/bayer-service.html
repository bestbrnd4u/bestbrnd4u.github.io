Can't find the bag you want in our catalog? We'll bring it from the US or Europe

If an authentic item isn't available in Ukraine or costs significantly less abroad, order it through BestBrnd4u. You find the item on the brand's official website or a marketplace, and we purchase it and deliver it to you, taking care of all the payment and logistics hassle.

What is the personal shopper service

It's a service that purchases items from foreign stores for customers in Ukraine. You choose any designer item on an official Western website — we buy it in our name, arrange international shipping and hand it over to you in Ukraine. It's not just bags and shoes: you can order clothing, accessories, cosmetics and other goods allowed for import this way.

💸

Sales and seasonal discounts on brands' official sites in the US and Europe often make buying cheaper even with shipping.

Wider selection

Models, colors and sizes that aren't on the Ukrainian market yet or have already sold out.

No logistics hassle

Just send us a link to the item — we'll handle payment to the overseas seller, shipping, and customs.

How it works

Six simple steps from a product link to the parcel in your hands.

1

You send a link to the item

Found the item you want on the brand's official website or a marketplace? Send us the link. Not sure where to look for a specific model? We'll recommend trusted stores ourselves.

2

Calculating the full cost

We tell you the final order total up front, with no hidden fees later:

  • 🏷️ the item price on the seller's website;
  • 📦 shipping cost to Ukraine;
  • 💰 service fee — 10–15% of the item price, depending on how complex the purchase is.
3

Make a prepayment

Once the price is agreed, you make a prepayment and we immediately purchase the item from the seller.

4

Choose a delivery method within Ukraine

Three options for different budgets and timelines:

Delivery option Price Time frame, days
🚚 From Europe from 280 UAH/kg 5–14
⛵ By sea from the US from 390 UAH/kg 20–45
✈ By air from the US from 560 UAH/kg 10–21
5

Track the status

You can check your order status at any time — message us on Instagram Direct or Telegram and we'll tell you where your parcel is.

6

Final payment and pickup

When the item arrives in Ukraine, you pay the balance and choose how to receive it — via Nova Poshta to any city in the country: to a branch, a parcel locker or by courier.
